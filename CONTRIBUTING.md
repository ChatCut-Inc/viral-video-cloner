# Contributing

Thanks for helping make Viral Video Cloner better.

## Share a recreation

Open a [showcase issue](https://github.com/ChatCut-Inc/viral-video-cloner/issues/new?template=showcase.yml)
with the original and your recreation side by side, the mode you used and, if you can, the plan.
Only share recreations of people who agreed and of sources you may use. Good ones are added to
[examples/](examples/README.md) with credit.

## Improve the skill

The skill lives in [`skills/viral-video-cloner/`](skills/viral-video-cloner). Pull requests that
make recreations more reliable are welcome. Please include:

- what went wrong before, with the source clip described (or linked, if public);
- what you changed in the instructions and why;
- one recreation made with the changed skill.

Keep the skill short and specific. Plain instructions an agent can follow beat long lists of rules.

Sign off each commit (`git commit -s`) to certify the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/). No CLA is needed.

## Report a problem

[Open a bug report](https://github.com/ChatCut-Inc/viral-video-cloner/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
with your agent (Claude Code or Codex) and its version, your ChatCut Desktop version, what you
asked for and what happened. Problems with your account or credits go to team@chatcut.io.
