# Pricing

- **The skill** is free and open source (Apache-2.0).
- **ChatCut Desktop** is free to download for macOS 13+ and Windows x64.
- **Recreations** are generated in the cloud and need a ChatCut Pro plan. They use the plan's
  credits ([pricing](https://chatcut.io/pricing?utm_source=github&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=oss_viral_cloner&install_source=oss_viral_cloner)).

## Typical cost

Measured on the examples in this repository (October 2026). Your agent always quotes the real
estimate before rendering.

| Length | Resolution | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 s test | 480p | ≈2 |
| 8 s | 720p | ≈9 |
| 10 s | 720p | ≈12 |
| 8 s | 1080p | ≈21 |

## Before every render

Generation starts the moment it is submitted and uses your credits, so your agent tells you the
length and the estimated credits first and waits for your go-ahead.
